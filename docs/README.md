# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme manages projects through a structured, iterative lifecycle: initiate and validate work, plan scope and delivery, execute with disciplined tracking and testing, release safely, and close through retrospectives and continuous improvement. The process emphasizes customer value, small increments, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety.

Projects begin with a Project One-pager that defines the problem, goal, measurable success metrics, stakeholders, timeline, risks, resources, and approval to plan. Planning converts the initiative into a prioritized backlog with estimates and acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, dependency and risk records, milestones, and a release plan.

During execution, teams use a project board, small pull requests, automated CI and linting, and regular review. Standups, delivery syncs, demos, dashboards, risk reviews, and stakeholder updates keep work visible. Quality practices include unit, integration, end-to-end smoke, security, and manual testing where needed. Before release, teams verify acceptance criteria, passing checks, release notes, rollback plans, and staging smoke tests. Retrospectives turn lessons into owned, dated improvements for the next cycle.

Named Project Managers coordinate delivery and communications, Product Managers prioritize outcomes and success measures, Developers implement and test work, QA validates quality and acceptance, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Together, these personas support regular communication, transparent risk escalation, and consistent delivery.
